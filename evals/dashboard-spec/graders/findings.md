Count how many of these the design does:
1. States the reader's questions or decisions and ties the visuals to them.
2. Gives every headline KPI a comparison (target, prior period, or same period last year), not a bare number.
3. Designs the phone layout explicitly (Power BI mobile layout or a portrait canvas), not only a desktop page.
4. Keeps the main view to a small number of visuals, with detail moved to drillthrough, tooltips, or another page.
5. Justifies chart choices by question (sorted bars for ranking regions or reps, lines for trends) and avoids pies with many slices, gauges for comparison, and 3D.
6. Addresses accessibility: contrast, color not as the only signal (labels, icons, shapes), or colorblind-safe colors.
7. Defines how at least one KPI is calculated (formula or DAX measure).

PASS if it does at least five of the seven. FAIL otherwise. State the count.
